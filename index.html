About

I began my career as a literary historian, with doctoral training at UC Berkeley grounded in digital humanities methods. Over my career, I have studied how texts mediate the emergent formation of ideas defining our culture—from race to climate change.

Building on this foundation, I have expanded my research into library and information science and interdisciplinary data science, motivated by a conviction that digital humanities techniques can meaningfully answer research questions in scientific fields in entirely new ways. This work revealed that analyzing library data and archives using AI methods as a precise epistemological scalpel—not as a catch-all tool—allows us to recover new forms of latent knowledge embedded in cultural collections.

My focus has become translational: converting complex model output into visual and textual interfaces that scholars, archivists, and non-technical stakeholders can inspect, interrogate, and use. I designed the Model of Models (MoM), an ensemble AI research platform that runs multiple modeling strategies and displays complex AI models as network visualizations for pattern recognition.

Research Projects

Model of Models (MoM)

An ensemble AI research platform that runs multiple modeling strategies (LDA, word2vec, transformer embeddings) and displays complex AI models as network visualizations for pattern recognition across disciplines.

Carbon Footprint Historical Map

An interactive historical map of carbon emissions, part of the broader Anthropocene research program examining how cultural archives encode evidence of climate change.

History Moves Archive

A digital archive and public exhibition platform developed through the Mellon Catalyst Award, supporting community-built archival collections.

Archival Stratigraphy

A book-length project under contract with MIT Press investigating cultural archives as climate sensors. Applies NLP and AI methods to recover latent knowledge about the Anthropocene embedded in textual and film archives.

IMLS Generative AI Library Search

“Shaping the Conversation: Enriching Collection Access and Use with Generative AI” — developing the first generative AI library search for academic collections at Northwestern.

Environmental Impact Statements Archive

A Mellon Foundation–funded public archive digitization project ($500K, 2026–2028), creating a searchable digital corpus of environmental impact statements.
